Question: How are client-uploaded documents handled to ensure security and compliance?
Answer: Security is paramount. All client-uploaded documents and scraped datasets are stored directly on your own secure cloud account (like Google Drive or AWS). They are protected by end-to-end encryption, which means no one outside of your company—not even the team at Advancify—can ever access the content. Only your authorized staff and the AI agent serving your business have permission to view them.
