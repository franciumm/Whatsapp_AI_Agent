### Category: Service Level & Contracts

**Question:** What are the general differences between the pricing tiers mentioned?
**Answer:** Our pricing tiers are designed to scale with your business's needs. The main differences are based on the volume of leads you process each month, the complexity and frequency of your data scraping requirements, and the number of advanced features you require, such as multi-platform integrations or advanced analytics.
/n/n

**Question:** What is the standard contract length? Is it a monthly or annual commitment?
**Answer:** Our standard agreement is a flexible month-to-month commitment, giving your business the freedom to adapt as your needs change without being locked into a long-term contract.
/n/n

**Question:** What is your cancellation policy?
**Answer:** You can cancel your subscription at any time. Since our service is month-to-month, the cancellation would simply take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

/n/n

**Question:** Is there any form of regional or industry exclusivity for founding partners?
**Answer:** Yes, this is one of the most significant benefits for our early adopters. We offer limited regional or niche exclusivity, meaning we will not onboard a direct competitor within your specific market sector or immediate geographical area, giving you a distinct and protected competitive advantage.
